Feeling Lazy or Exhausted?

The 3 Emergency Rules of Habit Preservation

1. The "Only 60 Seconds" Permission

Don't do the 45-minute workout. Just do 3 squats right where you stand. The goal isn't calorie burn today; the goal is not breaking the identity wire.

2. The "Never Miss Twice" Contract

Missing one day due to school work or tiredness is an accident. Missing two days in a row is the start of a new habit of quitting. Protect day 2 at all costs.

3. Self-Forgiveness Boost

Guilt creates paralysis. Accept yesterday as data, not failure. Complete a single micro-step right now to reboot.
